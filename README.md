# Hush

**English** · [한국어](README.ko.md)

**An Android browser that gives the screen to the page, without the ads and clutter.**

Hide anything in the way with one tap, remembered across the whole site. No account, no
analytics or ad SDKs.

1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
2. If asked, allow installing unknown apps.
3. Updates install the same way and keep your tabs, bookmarks and passwords.

## Highlights

- **More screen** — while you read, the address bar tucks into a small notch and the page runs
  to the edge.
- **Hide with a tap** — tap an ad or banner and it is gone on every page of that site.
- **Ad and tracker blocking** — on from the start.
- **Easier YouTube** — skips ads, keeps playing with the screen off, picks up where you
  stopped.
- **Privacy** — tracking codes taken out of links, incognito tabs that leave nothing behind.
- **Password manager** — encrypted on your phone, filled only on the right site.
