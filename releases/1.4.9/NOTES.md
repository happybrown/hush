**English**

- When an update is saved, the sheet now tells you that Android may ask you to allow your files app to install unknown apps. That is your files app's permission, not Hush's, and you can turn it off again after updating.
- The terms of use have a new section on updates: install Hush and its updates only from its GitHub release page. Hush never installs one itself.
- The privacy policy now also lists two permissions Hush already used, notifications (private tabs) and fingerprint or face unlock (saved passwords), and says which permissions are optional. The contact in both documents pointed to a Google Play page Hush does not have; it is now Hush's GitHub Issues. You will see a notice about the changes once.
